课题组成员萧星宇做国奖经验分享

发布日期：2025年04月28日

2025年4月25日，我校举办国奖分享会，直博二年级Xingyu Xiao同学作为国家奖学金得主，分享了学术心得。她强调好奇心与时间管理的重要性，并鼓励同学积极参与科研，提升学术能力。

On April 25, 2025, our university hosted a National Scholarship sharing session. Xingyu Xiao, a second-year direct Ph.D. student and National Scholarship recipient, shared her academic insights. She stressed the importance of curiosity and time management, encouraging students to engage in research and enhance their academic skills.
