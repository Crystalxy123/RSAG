欢迎沈绍宁、郭天远同学加入团队

Welcome Shen Shaoning and Guo Tianyuan to join the team

发布日期：2025年06月16日

2024年6月沈绍宁同学加入团队从事科研助理工作，将于2025年9月份开始攻读博士学位。 2024年9月郭天远同学加入团队攻读硕士学位。 欢迎两位加入团队！

In June 2024, Shen Shaoning joined the team to work as a research assistant and will start to work for a doctoral degree in September 2025. In September 2024, Guo Tianyuan joined the team to study for master's degree. Welcome to the team!
