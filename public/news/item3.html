祝贺！团队成员齐奔和李睿涵通过博士论文答辩

Congrats! Qi Ben & Li Ruihan passed PhD defenses.

发布日期：2025年06月16日

2025年5月9日，梁金刚副教授团队成员齐奔与李睿涵顺利通过博士学位论文答辩。齐奔的论文《高温气冷堆异常运行智能化分析 方法研究》面向 HTGR 异常运行场景，融合物理监测信息与深度学习技术，构建“异常监测–> 类型诊断–> 趋势预测–> 征兆分析”四维智能辅助决策体系，成果为多模块反应堆的 人机协同决策提供了理论范式与技术工具。李睿涵的《高保真度高温气冷堆多物理 耦合模拟及平衡堆芯源项分析》基于蒙特卡罗（MC）、计算 流体力学（CFD）以及离散元方法（DEM），建立了球床式高温气冷堆高保真中子 物理-热工-球流运动耦合模拟框架，成果为高温气冷堆的设计优化、安全评估等提供了重要理论支持。祝贺两位新晋博士！

On May 9, 2025, Qi Ben and Li Ruihan, members of Associate Professor Liang Jingang's research team, successfully defended their PhD dissertations. Qi Ben's dissertation, titled "Research on Intelligent Analysis Methods for Abnormal Operation of High-Temperature Gas-Cooled Reactors," focuses on abnormal operational scenarios in HTGRs. By integrating physical monitoring data with deep learning technology, he developed a four-dimensional intelligent decision support system encompassing "anomaly detection → fault diagnosis → trend prediction → precursor analysis." This work provides a theoretical paradigm and technical tools for human-machine collaborative decision-making in multi-module reactors. Li Ruihan's dissertation, "High-Fidelity Multi-Physics Coupling Simulation and Equilibrium Core Source Term Analysis of High-Temperature Gas-Cooled Reactors," establishes a high-fidelity coupled simulation framework for pebble-bed HTGRs based on the Monte Carlo (MC), Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), and Discrete Element Method (DEM). This research integrates neutronics, thermal-hydraulics, and pebble flow dynamics, providing significant theoretical support for the design optimization and safety assessment of HTGRs.
